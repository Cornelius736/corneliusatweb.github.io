Here are my standout projects:



>> Ballads of Henry Smith <<


My very first novel. Written in an experimental style where one chapter is told through the main character's journal entries, while the other is told through the eyes of an omnipresent narrator. The story follows Henry Smith, a war veteran from the American Civil War who fought tooth and nail for his rights and freedom, now in search of a quiet and peaceful life after the fact, only to be pulled into a feud between Cillian Finley and Aidan O'Connor. Check it out!



>> 1791 <<


The Republic of Potensland is a small but self-sufficient island nation in the Arctic Ocean, just above Svalbard. Why can't you find it on a map? Because the first-world powers are doing everything in their power to hide the mere existence of this great Republic from the public's eyes, claiming it to be an authoritarian regime. Of course, that is far from the truth, and it is you who are tasked with commanding a Regiment within the Custodia Republicana to defend this great Nation from foreign powers and traitorous rebels alike! Go forth, Colonel, and return victorious! Check it out!
