FaQ



>> What is your average response time?


I aim to respond to all emails within 24 hours; however, it may take longer if I'm busy or have a lot of emails to respond to.



>> Is PayHip trustworthy?


To my knowledge, yes. They are a UK-based company that has been around since 2012. They also have fast and direct support, which is always a good sign. Of course, if any issues with your purchase arise, contact me and we'll either sort it out or I'll issue a refund. And if PayHip is ever proven to be unreliable for the purpose, I'll look into other options.



>> What is the refund window?


All relevant information related to my Refund Policy, as well as my other Policies, can be found within the Legal article.



>> Are your projects open-source?


Generally, yes, as I'm a huge pro-activist of open-source work.



>> Why do you use AI in your (commercialized) projects?


I only use AI in my projects, commercial or otherwise, for productivity-related tasks. This includes writing code and troubleshooting, for example. I never have and never will use AI for creative tasks, such as writing, art, or music, for example. The key distinction here is that AI that is used for writing code generally sources the information it spits out from online documentation, which is made specifically for the purpose of being referenced when writing code. When AI is used for creative tasks, however, its output is usually a culmination of work from authors, artists, and the like who probably didn't consent to their work being used to train AI; thus, I will never use AI for such purposes.
