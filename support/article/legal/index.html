Privacy Policy


My websites, projects, and services (hereafter, "my work") do not directly collect any user data through means of cookies or other similar data collection or storage technologies. Some of my work (such as my A.P.M Web App) does use browser cache technology to store user information locally on the user's machine; as such, I do not have access to it.


Specific purposes, such as contact in the form of customer support and the keeping of purchase records at hand, may require me to occasionally access the following user information:


  • E-Mail
  • Name (whichever one the user gives me or is required)
  • Purchase Data:
    • Purchase ID
    • Receipt (if applicable)
    • Bank Statement Purchase Record (PDF format):
      • Store Name
      • Date Of Purchase
      • Exact Amount Spent
      • Last 4 Digits Of The Debit/Credit Card Used
    • Any other information that is relevant to the Purchase.
  • Any other information that the user provides me with.

None of this information is stored on my own servers, devices, or physical records; rather, said data is stored on the relevant platform, depending on purpose, making said platform the data controller of said user data. For all specific data-related concerns, inquiries, or requests, contact the relevant platform, as I have no administrative control over your user data.


To comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (hereafter, "GDPR") or any other Data Protection Laws And Regulations (hereafter, "DPLR"), I do not share any user data with any 3rd parties, regardless of purpose or circumstance, unless directly required to do so by law. Furthermore, I only retain access to the user data for as long as said user data is required (such as until the support ticket is closed), after which point, I terminate my access to that user data. I will, however, retain indefinite access to purchase data, as that data is required for various reports, records, refund requests, and other purposes.


This Policy may not, however, apply to 3rd party websites, projects, and services that are linked to, embedded on, or hosted by my work, such as my social media links and/or embeds. Likewise, this also applies to websites, projects, and services that host my work as well. I highly advise overviewing their respective Policies to learn how they use and handle user data.
