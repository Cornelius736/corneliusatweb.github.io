§I: Privacy Policy


First and foremost, I do not collect any user information that the user doesn't personally provide me with. However, some of my projects, products, and services (hereafter, "my work") may use certain data collection and storage technologies, such as how A.P.M stores user-entered data in the user's local browser cache to provide the necessary services. In this instance, I do not have access to this data unless the user directly provides me with it, and the user has absolute and unquestioned control over the data to view, modify, and even completely delete it at their own discretion without me ever being notified of any such changes.


Moreover, some of my services, such as my Store, are hosted on the 3rd-party platform, PayHip. This platform also utilizes cookies to save the user's login data, as well as stores the user's account data on their servers, which I do not have access to. The most that I can see in regards to the user's PayHip data is the information found on the purchase receipt, which may or may not include the following:


  • E-Mail.
  • Name (whichever one the user gives me or is required).
  • Purchase Data:
    • Purchase ID.
    • Receipt (if applicable).
    • Bank Statement Purchase Record (PDF format):
      • Store Name.
      • Date Of Purchase.
      • Exact Amount Spent.
      • Last 4 Digits Of The Debit/Credit Card Used.

        • PRO TIP: If the Bank Statement includes any irrelevant information, I advise redacting the same.


    • Any other information that is relevant to the Purchase.
  • Any other information that the user provides me with.

In all cases where I have access to any user data, such as support tickets, I will retain access to that data until the purpose of me having access to that data expires. In this specific example, until the support ticket is closed. During this time, referring to the period where I have access to the user's data, the user has full control to view, modify, or revoke my access to said data, which they can initiate by reaching out to me directly and requesting this data to be deleted. Once I receive the request, I will purge all of that user's data from any of my inboxes, files, folders, papers, or other places where data can be stored. This may not, however, apply when I do not have administrative access to the user's data, such as when said data is stored on PayHip's servers. In these cases, I can only revoke my own access to the data, not entirely purge that data from the servers. To completely purge that data from the servers, the user will need to contact the relevant platform.


In other cases, I may feature links on my work that lead to 3rd party platforms, such as social media or, as previously mentioned, 3rd party retailers (PayHip). In these cases, I am not responsible for how these 3rd party platforms handle user data, and I highly advise overviewing their individual legal documents to learn the same.


Importantly, I will never, under any circumstance or for any reason, share any user data that I have access to with any 3rd parties, unless it is strictly required for legal purposes. This may not apply for 3rd party platforms.


While neither I nor the relevant platform can guarantee full security of the user's data, in case of a data breach, I will cooperate with all relevant parties to identify the cause of the breach and resolve the issue as quickly as possible. If the cause of the breach is determined to be the relevant platform's negligence to properly store the user's data, I will look into alternative platforms for providing the same service(s) in the future.


Ultimately, I aim to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (hereafter, "GDPR") or any other data protection laws and regulations and will update this policy if I find that any points still remain questionable. In the meantime, the users are encouraged to file any complaints they may have with the relevant supervisory authority if they find my handling of their data or how I handle my policies to be concerning.
