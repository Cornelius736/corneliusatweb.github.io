Corn's Age Recommendation



* CAR (Corn's Age Recommendation) is not an official age rating but rather a general guideline for helping users decide whether my work is appropriate for their age group until or if it doesn't receive an official rating, such as an official PEGI rating, for example. CAR is not legally enforceable or backed by any organisation(s).


While not official or legally enforceable, CAR will still provide you with my honest assessment on the minimum age group recommended to access that particular piece of my work and what kinds of content can be found in the same. If you are a legal adult and do not agree with my recommendation, contact me.


The recommendations go as follows:


CAR GA:


General Audience


  • The item is appropriate for all ages.
  • No potentially disturbing and/or inappropriate imagery or language.
  • No substance use.

CAR GA+:


General Audience Plus


  • The item is appropriate for all ages.
  • The item does not show any disturbing and/or inappropriate imagery or language; however, it may suggest or imply it.
  • The item may contain casual substance (alcohol and tobacco) use.

CAR 12:


  • The item is only appropriate for ages 12 and above.
  • The item may feature some mild violence and/or semi-strong language.
  • The item may contain some heavier substance (alcohol and tobacco) use, such as slight drunkenness, often (but not necessarily always) used for comedic effect.

CAR 16:


  • The item is only appropriate for ages 16 and above.
  • The item may feature moderate violence and/or strong language.
  • The item may contain some heavier substance (alcohol and tobacco) use, such as heavy drunkenness or physical altercations caused by the same.

CAR M:


Mature


  • The item is only appropriate for ages 18 and above.
  • The item may feature violence, strong language, and some disturbing imagery and/or themes (some sexual content, substance abuse, drug use, etc.).

CAR M+:


Mature Plus


  • The item is intended exclusively for ages 18 and above; however, the disturbing imagery and/or strong language go beyond CAR M.
  • The item features disturbing imagery (sexual content, substance abuse, drug use, rape, body mutilation, etc.) and/or extremely strong language (such as racial slurs).

* Each page featuring my recommendation has a list noting which of these examples are featured in the specific item, as the above recommendations may include one, multiple, or all of them at the same time. Overviewing this list alongside checking my recommendation is highly advised to avoid certain content reaching inappropriate audiences.


* If the item contains gambling, microtransactions, or similar, it will be stated separately from the CAR.
