Ballads of Henry Smith

e-Book; Novel



Project Options:





Project Description:


My very first novel. Written in an experimental style where one chapter is told through the main character's journal entries, while the other is told through the eyes of an omnipresent narrator. The story follows Henry Smith, a war veteran from the American Civil War who fought tooth and nail for his rights and freedom, now in search of a quiet and peaceful life after the fact, only to be pulled into a feud between Cillian Finley and Aidan O'Connor.
