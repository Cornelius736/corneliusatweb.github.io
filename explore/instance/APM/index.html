A.P.M

Hello.



You just opened the A.P.M Web App! What is A.P.M, you ask? Well, "A.P.M" is short for Applicatio Productivitatis Multiusus. More specifically, A.P.M is a simple app designed to host a plethora of very useful and general tools that can easily be required in and applied to a wide variety of jobs and projects. That is precisely why ease of access is a key point of A.P.M.


Formerly, A.P.M was available for download on the Windows OS, built using Python. However, staying true to the key point, I decided to move the entire app onto the web, as it was not only easier for me to build and maintain (since I have far more experience with HTML, CSS, and JS than with Python), but it is also easier for other people to access it as well.


Use the navigation bar on the left to navigate the app. I am still working on a mobile port.



Basic Info:


  • All data (such as save files) are saved in your local browser's cache.
  • Internet access is required unless powered locally.
  • Version: 2.1-A
