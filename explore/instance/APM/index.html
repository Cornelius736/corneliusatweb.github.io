A.P.M

Hello.



A.P.M is a simple, productivity-oriented web app that features a plethora of different, general-use tools.


Use the navigation bar on the left (or top, if on mobile) to navigate the app.



Basic Info:


  • All data (such as save files) are saved in your local browser's cache.
  • Internet access is required unless powered locally.
  • Version: 2.4-B

Encryptor



(i) Enter your real message in the "Real Message" field. Then, enter 4 similar, but fake, ones in the "Fake Message" fields. After that, press "Encrypt." This will generate an encrypted version of your message, and it will also download (with permission) 5 Keys, only one of which is the correct Key. You can copy the text from the "Encrypted Message" field and move to the Decryptor.





























Decryptor



(i) You can paste your encrypted message in the "Encrypted Message" field below. Before decrypting, however, you will need to also upload one of the Keys you acquired earlier. Depending on which Key you upload, the decrypted result will be different. Since only one Key is correct, only one result is correct too. When both requirements are met, press "Decrypt."
