My favorite extraction shooters

Posted Oct 18, 2026. | ~8 min read.





Hi. I've been playing extraction shooters for almost a decade at this point, and I wanted to make this quick post outlining my favorites. That's pretty much it; hope you find something interesting or new to try out!


Just a quick note before I proceed: I want to clarify the definition of what games I consider to be extraction shooters, because it can technically be anything from Escape from Tarkov to Lethal Company (yes, I know how absurd it sounds to list a friendslop game as an extraction shooter, but it actually fits pretty decently into my requisites). Thus, I consider a game to be an extraction shooter if:


  1. You infil
  2. You can shoot guns
  3. You can loot, but if you die, you lose the loot
  4. You can extract

    5. (So, basically, the most literal definition imaginable.)


Going forward, I will present to you the game, give a barebones overview of the general gameplay loop, and list some positives and negatives. I won't go in-depth on different mechanics, visuals, maps, lore, setting, or anything like that, as the point of this post is to help you discover new games, not learn everything there is to know about them. If you'd like that, you can visit the official store page of each game.


* I am not being paid to promote these products.

* This list is in no particular order.

* ATW = At the Time of Writing



#1: HELLDIVERS II


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 177.1 hours


Monke Overview:

You drop in, you shoot enemies of Managed Democracy, you complete mission, and you extract (or don't).


✔ Fun

✔ Replayable

✔ You can play with friends

✘ A lot of the content is paywalled behind Warbonds *

✘ Lack of proper balancing between different weapons


* Yes, Super Credits can be earned through playing, but the rate is so slow that it might as well not even count. If you only farmed SC and did nothing else, it would take you between 5 and 10 hours for a single Warbond, assuming the RNG is on your side.



#2: Escape from Tarkov


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: ~140-ish hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you infil, you loot, you shoot, you complete mission, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ Decent graphics and sound design

✔ Guns feel very nice to use

✔ Can be fun

✔ Replayable

✔ Very in-depth

✘ Can be very annoying when you die to a bush camper

✘ Tedious fetch quests



#3: Grayzone Warfare


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 52.4 hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, heli flies you to an LZ, you loot, you shoot, you complete mission, and then heli flies you back to base. You died? Heli flies you back to the LZ, you loot your dead body, and then you win! :D


✔ Unique concept regarding taking helicopters to and from LZs

✔ Beautifully designed map

✔ Guns feel very nice to use

✘ Subpar looting *

✘ Subpar quests *


* Looting feels pointless, as most enemies have the same stuff on them and most things you find in the world are worthless. The developers did recently make an effort to address this, but it's still a pretty big downside.

* Most quests consist of going to a location, interacting with something, and going back to base. Either that, or you just have to kill a certain number of enemies. Very boring and placeholder-esque quest design.



#4: Hunt: Showdown


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 10.3 hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you infil, you shoot, you hunt bosses, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ Top-tier atmosphere

✔ Really good graphics and sound design

✔ Guns feel very nice to use

✔ Replayable

✘ Unbalanced matches *

✘ Server issues and rubber-banding


* While I know that some people don't really mind this, I absolutely hate it when games pair new players in the same game against veteran players, which Hunt: Showdown certainly does; it just ruins the enjoyment for everyone involved.



#5: Arena Breakout Infinite


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: ~50-ish hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you infil, you loot, you shoot, you complete mission, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ Good graphics and sound design

✔ Maps are well-built

✔ Guns feel very nice to use

✔ Fun

✔ Replayable

✘ Mobile game design *


* Since this was originally a mobile game, it makes sense why this would be the case, but the main menu is so bloated with random and unnecessary things that it negatively impacts the experience. Some character skins are also far too unrealistic and whacky for my taste.



#6: Incursion: Red River


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 40.4 hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you infil, you loot, you shoot, you complete mission, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ PvE only *

✔ Guns feel very nice

✔ Graphics and animations are actually very nice for an indie game

✔ Decent base building progression

✘ PvE only *

✘ Maps are very linear

✘ AI is really stupid *

✘ Sound design is completely whack *


* Depending on whether you prefer PvE or PvP, being exclusively locked to only PvE may either be a positive or a negative thing.

* You can customize AI, but these settings either make the AI dumb as rocks (my sincerest apologies to the rocks affected by this statement) or they have a literal aimbot; no in-between.

* An enemy that is 2 meters away from you sounds the same as an enemy that is 20 meters away from you. Also, directional audio is barely present.



#7: ZERO Sievert


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 21.9 hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you infil via train, you loot, you shoot, you complete mission, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ Cute top-down 2D art style

✔ In-depth weapon customization

✔ Decent progression

✔ Fun and replayable

✘ Weak story and plot *

✘ Questionable AI *


* The story is basically copied over from the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. franchise with the names of certain places and characters changed.

* Sometimes, the AI will start shooting you from outside your FOV; since the game is top-down 2D, that is a pretty big problem.



#8: Road to Vostok


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 7.2 hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you exit safehouse, you loot, you shoot, and you get back to your safehouse (or don't).


✔ Decent visuals and sound design

✔ Weapons feel very good to use

✔ Base building is a very cool feature

✔ Permadeath *

✘ Permadeath *

✘ Tedious looting *

✘ OP AI *

✘ Sound design is a little wonky *


* Late-game regions have permadeath, where if you die, the game deletes your entire save file. Depending on whether you like the massive risk, this may be a positive or negative.

* So many containers that you open will be completely empty, but then a random fuse box will have a whole treasure trove of things in it. This makes looting feel really unsatisfying.

* The AI can see you from extremely far away (so far that sometimes their bodies are literal dots on the screen), usually through foliage, and they can actually hit you pretty consistently through and from the aforementioned.

* While not as bad as with Incursion: Red River, it's still sometimes pretty hard to accurately tell from where an enemy is coming from.



#9: Lethal Company


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 109.5 hours


Monke Overview:

You land, you explore abandoned facility to find scrap, you collect scrap, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ Cool visual style and very good sound design

✔ Extremely fun and replayable

✔ Decent horror experience

✔ Really cool atmosphere

✘ Some enemies are a little bit unbalanced *


* Some enemies, such as the Giant Sapsucker or the Kidnapper Fox, can completely screw over your run if they happen to spawn near your ship or a main route to and from the facility. And there really isn't much you can do to either of them either.


You are also probably wondering what exactly makes Lethal Company a shooter? Well, there's this little guy called the Nutcracker, and if you kill him, you can grab and use his shotgun, which makes Lethal Company technically a shooter, even if that isn't the main focal point.



#10: HOLE


Get it on Steam


My playtime ATW: 9.5 hours


Monke Overview:

You gear up, you infil, you loot, you shoot, you complete mission, and you exfil (or don't).


✔ Very cool atmosphere and liminal vibe

✔ Very fun

✔ The liminal maps have a really good, and sometimes creepy, backrooms-esque look

✔ Very unique gameplay mechanics regarding weapons overheating and having to manually pull the slide

✔ Weapons feel extremely good to use

✘ Not really replayable *

✘ Not a whole lot of content *


* The missions are pretty barebones, usually consisting of either killing a set number of enemies or collecting a set number of different items. There's also no real incentive to replay the game because of this.

* There's only a handful of maps, base building is really simple, and you can unlock everything within 7 to 8 hours of playtime.



CONCLUSION


And that just about wraps yet another post up. Hope you found something new, and until next time, cheers! :p
