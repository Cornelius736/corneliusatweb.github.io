Projects


Over the years, I've worked on a plethora of different things; here are my current projects.



>> Ballads of Henry Smith <<


My very first novel. Written in an experimental style where one chapter is told through the main character's journal entries, while the other is told through the eyes of an omnipresent narrator. The story follows Henry Smith, a war veteran from the American Civil War who fought tooth and nail for his rights and freedom, now in search of a quiet and peaceful life after the fact, only to be pulled into a feud between Cillian Finley and Aidan O'Connor.





>> 1791 <<


The Republic of Potensland is a small but self-sufficient island nation in the Arctic Ocean, just above Svalbard. Why can't you find it on a map? Because the first world powers are doing everything in their power to hide the mere existence of this great Republic from the public's eyes, claiming it to be an authoritarian regime. Of course, that is far from the truth, and it is you who are tasked with commanding a Regiment within the Custodia Republicana to defend this great Nation from foreign powers and traitorous rebels alike! Go forth, Colonel, and return victorious!





>> A.P.M <<


Short for Applicatio Productivitatis Multiusus, A.P.M is a simple, yet comprehensive toolbox featuring many general-purpose tools, such as a To-Do List, Color Picker, Unit Converter, Ruler, Auto Clicker, and many more!





>> C.A.P <<


Short for "Corn's Anomaly Plus," this is a relatively light-weight modpack for S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Anomaly, which aims to enhance the default experience of Anomaly without compromising on the stability of the same.
